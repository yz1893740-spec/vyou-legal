Vyou Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

Vyou is an app that helps you hand your phone to someone else to take a photo. This notice explains what it does with your data, and what it does not do.

In one sentence

Vyou has no server and no account. Your photos never leave your phone.

Photos

Photos you take are stored on your phone.

Photos are never uploaded to a developer server — because Vyou has no server.

No live viewing

While your phone is in someone else's hands, no one else can see the camera view from anywhere else. The view exists only on that phone.

No account

Vyou needs no sign-up, no login, and no cloud sync. There is no account in the settings page because there really is none.

Third parties

Vyou does not use any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK from a third party. No third party receives your data, because no data is ever sent outside the App.

There is one exception, and it is not "handing your data to a company":

  • The App Store. Purchases are handled by Apple, and that part is covered by Apple's own privacy policy. Vyou never sees or keeps your payment details.

Permissions the App needs, and how to revoke them anytime

Vyou asks for two system permissions, each used only for exactly what it says:

  • Camera — to take photos.
  • Photos (Add Only) — to add the original photos you choose to keep into the system photo library. This permission can only add — it cannot read or delete photos already in your library.

Both can be revoked at any time: open the iOS Settings app, find Vyou, and turn the permission off. The matching feature stops working, but photos already on your phone are left untouched.

Other than these two permissions, Vyou collects no other data that needs your consent, so there is no other consent to withdraw.

Diagnostic logs

The App keeps a technical log on the device (error codes, version, the steps you took) to look into problems when they happen.

  • It stays on your phone only, and is never sent anywhere.
  • It contains no photo content, and no personal data that identifies you.
  • It is kept for at most 7 days, and is protected by iOS file protection.
  • You can see its current contents under "Home → Debug info."

What stays on your phone

Session records and the App's temporary photos stay on the device.

"Clear cache" in the settings page only clears the App's own temporary files — it never touches files already saved to the Photos app.

Purchases

Vyou's paid plans are day passes (7 days, 15 days, or 6 months), a one-time purchase that never auto-renews. Whether to buy again after one expires is entirely up to you. Payment is handled entirely by the App Store; Vyou never sees or keeps your payment details.

A pass starts counting down only from its first activation — buying one and leaving it unused never expires it. The activation time is stored only in the App’s local data on this phone. It is currently not synced through iCloud; the developer has no server and cannot see it.

After switching phones or reinstalling, "Restore Purchases" with the same Apple ID queries your App Store purchase records. Activation times stay on the original installation, so the original remaining pass time may not carry over for now.

Your rights

Because Vyou does not collect or keep your personal data, there is no data for you to ask us to access, correct, or delete. You control what is on your own phone: deleting the App removes every local record and temporary photo.

Children

Vyou is not designed for children and does not collect any age information.

When this notice changes

Any material change updates the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
