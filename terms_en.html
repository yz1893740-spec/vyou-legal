Vyou Purchase Terms

Last updated: 2026-09-29

The formal terms are Apple's Standard EULA. This page is a plain-language explanation; Apple's original text is linked at the bottom.

What you are buying

Vyou's paid plans are day passes: 7 days, 15 days, or 6 months. A one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it never auto-renews. All advanced features are unlocked for the pass period; after it expires you return to the free version. Whether to buy again is always your own decision.

A pass starts immediately after purchase, using the App Store purchase time plus its days × 24 hours, without rounding to midnight. Additional passes queue after existing coverage. The purchase and pass pages show local start and end times. Refunded passes are excluded.

Payment and refunds

Payment is handled by the App Store; Vyou never sees or keeps your payment details.

Refunds must be requested from Apple. Once a refund completes, that pass is voided and features return to the free version.

Switching phones or reinstalling

Restore Purchases with the same Apple ID rereads App Store transactions, without a Vyou account. Pass windows are rebuilt only from transactions; switching phones or reinstalling never extends or resets them.

The free version is always there

You can take and save photos without Pro. A failed purchase, a cancelled purchase, or the store being unreachable never affects any of that.

Apple's Standard Terms

When a developer does not provide custom terms, an App Store app is automatically covered by Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement. The original text is at:
